# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-07-27

First public release of Olivia.

### Included

- Direct and Interview planning through Squad, with editable JSON plans and preview-before-Build.
- Additive ProcessWire field, template, page, content and frontend generation.
- Detached workers with deadlines, single-flight protection and visible job diagnostics.
- Persistent chats, Build History and manifest-based Undo.
- Reference URL, notes and screenshot analysis, including optional ScreenshotOne capture.
- A validated catalog of 119 website components and curated deterministic blueprints.
- First-class Landing page, Business website, Catalog and Online store contracts and blueprints.
- Searchable, category-filtered catalogs with detailed product galleries and per-product enquiries.
- Fail-closed Mercato integration for real cart, checkout, payments, orders, discounts, fulfilment and inventory.
- Email-verified customer registration, sign-in and Mercato-backed order history for generated stores.
- Responsive generated views with bundled CSS, navigation, SEO metadata, Open Graph data and a native contact form.
- Optional AI content filling, image generation, Atlas indexing and Ichiban integration.
- Module discovery, learned module skills and approval-gated installation recommendations.
- Secret-free support diagnostics, local opt-in telemetry and release/package verification tooling.

### Safety

- Plans are normalized and validated before Build.
- Builds are reversible and fail closed when their rollback manifest cannot be saved.
- Slow or stalled providers cannot hold a web request or generator indefinitely.
- Optional Atlas failures remain visible as warnings without failing the primary Build.
- API keys, credentials, reference images and customer content are excluded from support bundles and telemetry.

This is the first published Olivia version; there are no earlier public releases.
